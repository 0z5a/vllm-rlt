# PR17 review disposition

The one-off Q2 benchmark controller, driver, schema, report and qualification
modules were removed at the owner's request, together with their tests,
contract fixtures and procedural documentation. The reusable cached Ouro
reference adapter and its focused tests remain.

The historical [BF16 results and raw evidence](../benchmarks/q2-external-bf16-20260912.md)
are retained. They describe the recorded execution revision, not a supported
benchmark command in the current tree.

The canonical FP32 timing decision remains the
[final qualification record](evidence/q2-external-final-qualification.json),
backed by the unchanged [chronology](evidence/q2-external-chronology.json).
It preserves generation equivalence while marking timing controls invalid and
performance qualification inconclusive. Removing the harness does not qualify
those historical timings.
