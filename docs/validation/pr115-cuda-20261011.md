The new CUDA cases execute two real queued speculative rounds, with actual compute/copy streams and bank leases:

- Two snapshots in one serving tick, including the terminal snapshot, deliver the entire committed suffix.
- A decoder failure after retirement remains local to that request; a fresh healthy request completes through the same CUDA engine and serving adapter.
- An invalid completed frontier preserves the primary exception and releases every pending bank and KV allocation.
- A second unreadable host payload is discarded without decoding it or replacing the original frontier exception.

Both successful and failing decoder paths are exercised. Both readable and unreadable second payloads are exercised. Existing regressions cover FP32/BF16 parity, ragged limits, prefix reuse, cancellation, partial submission failures, EOS, request-ID reuse, multi-stream close, and full-depth KV parity.
