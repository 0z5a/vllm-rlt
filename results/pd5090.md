# Speculative PD E2E on RTX 5090

Equal-card comparison: two independent speculative replicas versus one prefill/decode pair on two RTX 5090s. Both arms use Ouro-1.4B, BF16, Triton, `last_exited`, d=2/D=4, K=4, greedy decoding, and 32 output tokens per request. Five alternating paired trials follow one warmup per arm. Timings exclude model startup and include prefill, NIXL transfer, speculative decoding, and KV commit. Every output token and exit depth matched. Values are median ± median absolute deviation (seconds); speedup is replica median / PD median.

| Requests | Prompt tokens | Two replicas, s | 1P1D, s | PD speedup | Raw trials |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 4 | 128 | 1.788 ± 0.101 | 2.732 ± 0.036 | 0.654× | [JSON](rtx5090/raw.json) |
| 4 | 512 | 2.296 ± 0.104 | 6.828 ± 0.156 | 0.336× | [JSON](rtx5090-p512/raw.json) |

The PD path is correct on these workloads but slower than two replicas. The result does not imply a PD speedup at other arrival rates or request mixes.
