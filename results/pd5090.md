## Supplemental RTX PRO 6000 qualification

On a separate two-card RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition instance, the updated PD code passed 13 CPU tests and nine two-card tiny-Ouro GPU tests, including signal-free worker-failure shutdown. One four-card test was skipped. The instance used its preinstalled `/venv/main`; no packages were changed.

Ouro-1.4B full-model E2E compared two independent replicas with 1P1D on the same two GPUs. BF16, Triton, LAST_EXITED, fixed four loops, greedy sampling, and identical prompts were used. Every trial's output tokens and exit depths matched exactly. Each arm had a warmup and three alternating timed trials; model startup was excluded, while prefill, transfer, and decode were included.

| Workload (4 requests each) | Two replicas median s | 1P1D median s | PD speedup | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 256 prompt / 32 output tokens | 2.067 | 2.249 | 0.919× | PASS, PD slower |
| 256 prompt / 128 output tokens | 6.773 | 7.506 | 0.902× | PASS, PD slower; GPU 1 had another worker during a later trial |

The [32-token raw trials](pro6000/raw.json) and [128-token raw trials](pro6000-w1/raw.json) include prompts, individual times, output tokens, and exit depths. Their generated tables are [32 tokens](pro6000/speedup.md) and [128 tokens](pro6000-w1/speedup.md). These two-card RTX PRO 6000 measurements do not populate the RTX 5090 table above or establish a PD speedup on the target machine.
